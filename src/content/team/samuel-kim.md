Hi! I first became interested in neuroscience as a musician, wondering if recording my brain
activity can retrieve my pop song ideas that I can't record with one instrument at a time. Although
that is not currently possible, the mouse auditory cortex and techniques for recording its activity
are also really amazing. In the lab, I work with Oliver to extract representations of sound from
the activity of cortical neurons in mice, specifically spatial information. Outside the lab, I'm
training to become the greatest unpaid ambidextrous multi-sport athlete of all time.
