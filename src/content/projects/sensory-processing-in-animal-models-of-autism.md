One of the most common phenotypes present in individuals with autism includes sensory
hypersensitivity and difficulty hearing in complex sensory environments. Genetic studies have
revealed several genetic mutations that are highly correlated with ASD.

In our lab we use a mouse model of Fragile X Syndrome, the Fmr1-knockout mouse line, to study sensory
encoding in complex environments. We utilize various imaging techniques including calcium imaging,
fiber photometry, and optogenetics to dissect the neural circuitry recruited in auditory processing
and how it differs in the mouse model of ASD.

Currently we are investigating how the cholinergic system could potentially play a role in the
auditory processing impairments noted by ASD patients.
