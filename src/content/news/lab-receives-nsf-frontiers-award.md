---
title: Lab receives NSF Frontiers award
date: 2023-09-01
summary: A four-year NSF Neural and Cognitive Systems grant to study how the brain performs complex scene analysis.
---

The lab received a 4-year National Science Foundation Neural and Cognitive Systems grant to study how
the brain performs Complex Scene Analysis. This exciting collaboration with Drs. Kamal Sen, David
Boas, and Virginia Best will be important for developing next-generation assisted hearing devices.
