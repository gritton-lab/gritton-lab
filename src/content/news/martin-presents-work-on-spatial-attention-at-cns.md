Martin presented his work from our collaboration with Sepideh Sadaghiani's
[CONNECT lab](https://connectlab.beckman.illinois.edu/) on the role of alpha and gamma rhythms in
auditory spatial attention at the
[2024 Cognitive Neuroscience Society meeting](https://www.cogneurosociety.org/annual-meeting/) in
Toronto.
